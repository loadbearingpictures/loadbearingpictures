Wu Wei: After the Fall

Short Film · Narrative Documentary · Post-Production

After a severe head injury changes her relationship with movement, Canadian parkour pioneer Mandy Lam sets out to reclaim her body, her practice and her place in an art she has helped shape for decades.

Starring: Mandy Lam

Director/Writer: Mandy Lam and Brennan Martin

This film is supported by the Canada Council for the Arts.
